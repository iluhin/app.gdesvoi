## How it works

Choose a city and enter its chat. More than 170 cities are available: all major
cities and all regional capitals, from Murmansk to Krasnodar and from Kaliningrad
to Vladivostok. You can detect your city from your location with one tap.

| | |
|---|---|
| 💬 | public city chat and private messages, with one-click replies |
| ❤️ | message reactions and likes on profiles |
| 💞 | the 18+ “Знакомства” (“Dating”) section with profiles and mutual likes |
| 📸 | a profile with large photos, a circular avatar, emoji, and a background for your city |
| 📎 | photos, videos, and PDFs directly in messages |
| 👥 | see who is online and who is away |
| 🤖 | an AI chat companion: address it by name and it “types” and replies |
| 🎨 | light and dark themes and several color schemes |

To register, enter your name, email address, and password; a code sent by email
confirms your address. If the administrator has enabled guest access, you can
take a look without registering.

## Android app

- **Feels like a native app.** A branded animated splash screen while the chat
  loads in the background; “Назад” (“Back”) closes windows and sections; a
  dedicated offline screen automatically opens the chat when the connection returns.
- **Everything available on the website.** Attachments and photos from your
  gallery, city detection, dating, and your profile — using the same account as
  on the website.
- **No unnecessary permissions.** Internet and approximate location — only via
  the “Определить город” (“Detect city”) button. The store installs updates.
- **Lightweight.** About 100 KB, with no ads or trackers.
- **Requires** Android 7.0 or later.

Get the file [from the releases page](../../releases) or directly
[from the website](https://gdesvoi.ru/update.php?a=download) — all versions are
signed with the same key, so updates install over the existing version.

## iPhone and desktop

There is no separate iPhone app, but the website is a PWA: “Поделиться” (“Share”)
→ “На экран „Домой“” (“Add to Home Screen”) gives you an icon and launches it
without an address bar. In Chrome on Android and desktop, use the “⋮” menu →
“Установить приложение” (“Install app”), or the “Установить приложение”
(“Install app”) option in the chat's profile menu.
